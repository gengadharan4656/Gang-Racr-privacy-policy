## 1. Introduction

Gen Soft Tech ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how GaNg RaCe handles information when you use our mobile game.

GaNg RaCe is a racing game designed to provide an enjoyable gaming experience. We aim to minimize the collection of personal information and do not require users to create an account or sign in to play.

## 2. Information We Collect

In the current release, GaNg RaCe is designed not to collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers.

We do not intentionally collect:

- Names or personal identification details.
- Email addresses or phone numbers.
- Account credentials or passwords.
- Location information for tracking purposes.
- Personal contacts, photos, or messages.

## 3. Local Wi-Fi Multiplayer

GaNg RaCe may provide local Wi-Fi multiplayer functionality.

When using this feature, devices may exchange necessary network information, such as local IP addresses, connection details, and gameplay information, to establish and maintain a multiplayer session.

This information is used for communication between participating devices. It is not intentionally transmitted to Gen Soft Tech servers for collection or tracking.

Your network provider and other devices on your local network may independently process network-related information.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

If third-party services are introduced in future versions, this policy will be updated to explain their data practices.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

GaNg RaCe is designed to operate without requiring a user account or centralized personal-data storage.

Certain game settings and gameplay-related information may be stored locally on your device.

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic communication or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain a personal user database or retain personal information on our own servers in the current release.

Locally stored game settings and other application data can generally be removed by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the application.

Network information exchanged during local multiplayer is used for the active connection and is not intentionally retained by us.

## 7. Children's Privacy

GaNg RaCe does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can investigate and take appropriate action.
